# 07 — Dashboard teaser sections: recent players + active tournaments

**What to build:** The Dashboard (below the stat cards, above the actions) shows two sections. "Recent players" lists up to three of the most recently added players, each card showing the player's name and the disciplines they can play; tapping a card opens that player's edit flow. "Active tournaments" lists up to three of the most recently created active tournaments, each card showing name, discipline, format, teams filled out of target, and status; tapping a card opens that tournament. A section with nothing to show renders a one-line invite (create a tournament / add a player) instead of hiding. Both sections stay community-scoped and follow the active community on switch.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Dashboard teaser helpers (the sections render the helpers' output).
